Privacy Policy for Wusa

App name: Wusa

This Privacy Policy explains how Wusa (“we”, “us”, or “the app”) handles information when you use the Wusa iOS application. By using Wusa, you agree to this Policy.

1. Overview

Wusa is a literature-focused social app where readers build a reading persona, browse and publish stories in Novel Salon, match with readers who share similar taste, chat (including optional photos and voice notes), complete an intentionality assessment, and unlock dossier features with consumable Credits purchased through Apple.

2. Information we process

Depending on how you use Wusa, we may process:

2.1 Account information

  • Username and password (or Sign in with Apple identifiers) used to create and access your account
  • Display name and optional profile bio or portrait you choose to save
  • Session tokens used to keep you signed in and to sync recent chats

2.2 Reading taste and matching

  • Gender, era circles, story-time settings, and genre tags you select in Reading Taste
  • Favorite novels and characters on your Novel Shelf
  • Assessment answers and generated dossier versions when you complete the intentionality assessment

2.3 Community and chat content

  • Posts, titles, tags, reflections, comments, and media you publish to Novel Salon
  • Chat threads and messages you send or receive, including text, image, and voice notes
  • Moderation actions such as reports, blocks, and hidden posts

2.4 Purchases

  • Consumable Credit pack purchases processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store your full payment card details. Apple provides purchase receipts and transaction status to unlock Credits in the app.

2.5 Device and technical data

  • On-device preferences and flags (for example, whether onboarding or the notification prompt has been shown)
  • Crash-adjacent system diagnostics that iOS may provide to you or to Apple; Wusa does not operate a separate advertising tracker

3. How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • Authenticate you and maintain your session
  • Personalize Match recommendations from your Reading Taste persona
  • Operate Novel Salon, chats, assessment dossiers, and PDF preview/export
  • Sync recent chat history across your devices signed into the same account
  • Process Credit purchases and unlock paid dossier features
  • Enforce community standards (filter, report, block, hide, and developer follow-up)
  • Respond when you contact us at wusaliterature@gmail.com

4. Facial data

4.1 What we collect

Wusa does not create biometric faceprints, face templates, or Face ID–style identity maps. If you choose to capture or select a profile portrait, story photo, chat image, or short video, those media may depict a face.

4.2 Planned use

Optional photos and videos that may depict a face are used only to:

  • Display your chosen profile portrait inside Wusa
  • Attach images or short clips to Novel Salon posts or chat messages you send
  • Support authenticity / motion-check UI surfaces that show media you provide

We do not use facial imagery for advertising profiling, emotional analysis for sale, or biometric authentication against a face database.

4.3 Sharing, disclosure, and where stored

  • Profile portraits and story media you keep on the device are stored in the app’s sandboxed documents area.
  • Chat images and voice notes you send may be uploaded to our cloud storage so recent chats can sync to other devices signed into your account.
  • We do not sell facial imagery. Disclosure is limited to service infrastructure needed to operate Wusa, or when required by law.

4.4 Retention

  • Device-held portraits and media remain until you replace them, delete the related content, use in-app data erasure, or delete the app.
  • Synced chat media follows the chat retention window described in Section 6 (recent chats are kept for a limited period on our servers).

4.5 Scope of this chapter

This Section 4 is the complete explanation of Wusa’s collection, use, disclosure, sharing, and retention practices for facial data (including optional user photos that may depict a face). Wusa does not collect biometric faceprints.

5. Permissions

Wusa requests the following iOS permissions when you use related features:

Permission When Why
Camera Capturing portraits, story photos/video, or chat images Create media inside Wusa
Photo Library Choosing existing portraits, story images/videos, or chat images Attach media you already have
Microphone Voice notes in chat and speech-to-text while writing reflections Record audio and dictate text
Speech Recognition Dictation in Novel Studio Convert speech into text on device where available
Notifications First time you reach the main Home experience Deliver notifications related to features you use in Wusa

You can revoke permissions at any time in iOS Settings. Some features will not work without the related permission.

6. Storage, sync, and retention

  • Account credentials and authentication are handled through Wusa’s backend services.
  • Reading taste, shelf, Credits balance, assessment drafts/records, and many preferences are stored on your device.
  • Community prompts and public salon content may be loaded from Wusa’s backend; posts you create can also be stored on device overlays used by the app.
  • Chat threads and messages from approximately the last seven (7) days may be synced to Wusa’s servers so you can continue conversations on another device with the same account. Older local copies may remain on a device until cleared.
  • Report dossier PDFs may be generated through Wusa cloud functions or a local fallback, then previewed or shared by you.

7. Push notifications

Wusa may send notifications related to the features you use in the app, according to how the app works. You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings. Wusa requests notification authorization with the system dialog when you first reach Home; there is no separate in-app “Enable Notifications” button.

8. In-app purchases

Credits are consumable virtual items sold through Apple In-App Purchase. Payment processing is handled by Apple. Refunds are governed by Apple’s policies. Wusa does not store payment card numbers.

9. Community moderation and reports

When you report content, Wusa can hide the material for you, record block actions, and open a mail draft to wusaliterature@gmail.com with a content snippet so we can review. We aim to act on reports within 24 hours. Blocking prevents further interaction with the blocked author in the experiences Wusa provides.

10. Third-party services

Wusa uses infrastructure providers to host APIs, storage, and PDF generation used by the app. Purchase processing uses Apple. We do not sell personal information to data brokers. If a feature sends user content to a third-party AI provider, that path will be disclosed in an update to this Policy before activation; the current shipping build does not require a third-party generative-AI API for core Matching, Salon, chat, or assessment flows.

11. Delete All Data and Sign Out

  • Sign Out ends your active session on the device while keeping account records available for a later Sign In.
  • Erase / Delete All Data (Profile) permanently removes user-owned content and preferences held on the device (including assessments, local posts overlays, Credits balance, media files Wusa stored for you, and related flags). Synced server-side chat rows associated with your account may also be removed or become inaccessible after account-level deletion requests sent to us at wusaliterature@gmail.com.

12. Children’s privacy

Wusa is intended for users 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a minor has provided information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

13. No sale of personal data

We do not sell your personal information.

14. International users

Wusa may process information in regions where our service providers operate. By using the app, you understand that information may be transferred to those regions under appropriate safeguards.

15. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised Policy. The current Policy is also available in the app via Privacy.
